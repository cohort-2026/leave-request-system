End-to-end user flow

From the planning review (25 Aug 2026). Click a step to open that screen.

→ → → → → →

Employee

Self-onboard, submit leave, see own requests / calendar / balance, cancel while pending.

Manager

Team queue, same working-day total, approve / reject (reason required), team calendar, block team dates.

HR / Company Admin

Own company only: calendar, holidays, leave types, employees, onboarding link, company date blocks.

Platform Admin

Creates companies. Does not use another company’s leave data day-to-day.

Access rule: every user belongs to one company except Platform Admin. Nobody sees another company’s employees, leave, or configuration.

Log in

Official channel for leave. Employees do not self-register here — they use a company onboarding link.

Wireframe shortcut: four login buttons so each role can be walked through. Production has one login; role comes from the account.
